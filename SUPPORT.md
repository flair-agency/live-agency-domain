# Support

For questions about LIVE Agency Domain source, documentation, or proposed improvements, open an [issue](https://github.com/flair-agency/live-agency-domain/issues). Include the relevant commit, the affected business context or contract, and a clear question or expected outcome. Use publishable synthetic examples.

This public development monorepo is still undergoing hosted qualification. Requests do not establish product support, a release commitment, a service-level agreement, or a guaranteed response time.

For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include private platform interpretation, company mappings, selected resource identifiers, credentials, or real operational data in a public support request.
