# Knowledge and implementation ownership

This repository's public responsibility is cross-platform LIVE agency meaning and use cases whose disclosure has been reviewed. Public Skills may implement those use cases and express their business decisions. A capability contract describes what a use case needs without making a particular service or tool its business authority.

Determine semantic ownership and disclosure rights separately. Concrete platform meaning and its correspondence to shared concepts belong with the platform owner. Company-specific choices and service mappings belong with the appropriate organization owner. A selected environment owns physical resource, identity, permission, and version bindings. A Provider owns service operations and representation normalization. Runtime or another explicitly selected composition owns the current environment's access path. A Plugin or MCP tool is an implementation option, not a business decision maker.

Private owners remain independent sibling repositories. Public checkout and its basic checks must not require their Gitlinks or source checkouts. A selected composition uses reviewed contracts and fixed artifact versions. Repository location alone never chooses a production route or authorizes an effect.

Publish only content with a recorded source and permitted disclosure. Keep protected meaning, operational evidence, real data, identifiers, and credentials with their authorized owners. A generic rewrite of restricted source knowledge is not, by itself, publication clearance.
