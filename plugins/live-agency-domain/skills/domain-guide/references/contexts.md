# LIVE agency business contexts

This document describes common agency concepts. It does not specify a particular LIVE platform, service field, resource, source credential, or current deployment.

## Creator Scouting

Creator Scouting discovers, evaluates, approaches, and tracks prospective LIVE creators. A scouting case ends when agency membership is confirmed or the case closes without membership. Observations about a creator and the agency's decision to approach that creator are different facts. A missing or unavailable invitation-eligibility observation does not establish ineligibility.

## Creator Management

Creator Management covers affiliated creators from confirmed membership through departure or the end of management. It includes creator activity, management work, incentives and rewards, support, development cost, and management performance analysis. When membership is confirmed and the scouting case closes as joined, observations of the creator can continue for management purposes.

## Expense and Accounting

Expense and Accounting records actual monetary expenditure, receipts, expense candidates, applications, and accounting registration. An estimated value attributed to a creator or business purpose is not automatically an accounting expenditure. Cross-context exchanges must identify their source and authority so the contexts do not maintain conflicting versions of one authoritative state.

## Information boundaries

Keep a person, an account, an agency affiliation, and a technical record identity distinct. Keep platform-observed relationships distinct from agency contractual relationships. A source may provide an observation; the owning business context decides how to use it. Concrete source labels, matching rules, mappings, and selected records belong to their reviewed owners.
