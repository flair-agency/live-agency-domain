# Development agent instructions

- Follow [contributor guidance](docs/governance/contributing.md) and [ownership](docs/architecture/ownership.md). Read the owning business contract before changing a Skill or capability.
- Preserve the distinction among Creator Scouting, Creator Management, and Expense/Accounting. Do not turn source observations or estimates into agency decisions or accounting facts.
- Choose the semantic owner and permitted disclosure independently. Keep concrete platform interpretation, company service mappings, selected resource IDs, credentials, real data, and restricted provenance with their appropriate owners.
- Treat private sibling repositories as independent sources. Do not introduce Gitlinks or require a private checkout for public-source checks. Reference reviewed contracts and selected artifact versions where a composition needs a private sibling.
- Inspect the working tree before edits, preserve unrelated work, and review the scoped diff. Run focused checks and report their actual results and limits. An available tool or ambient session does not authorize an external operation.
