# Code of Conduct

Participate respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and perspectives, focus feedback on the work, and give and receive constructive criticism. Respect other contributors' privacy and the repository's [ownership boundaries](docs/architecture/ownership.md).

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may moderate, edit, or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community.

For a concern visible in a public contribution, link that contribution in an [issue](https://github.com/flair-agency/live-agency-domain/issues) only when doing so does not expose sensitive information. A verified private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been published. Retain sensitive details privately until maintainers establish one; do not post personal data or confidential allegations publicly. Security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
